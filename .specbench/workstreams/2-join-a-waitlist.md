# Join a waitlist

## Goals

- Bookers can join a waitlist for a time that is already booked
- When a time frees up, the first person in line is emailed and can claim it within the offer window
- If they don't claim it in time, the next person in line gets the offer
- Organizers switch the waitlist on per Event Type and choose the offer window

## Out of scope

- Paid Event Types
- Recurring series
- Webhooks for waitlist events
- Showing the Organizer who is on the waitlist
